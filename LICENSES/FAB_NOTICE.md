## What this means

The Fab Standard License explicitly permits **commercial distribution of
projects that incorporate Fab assets** — i.e., bundling the model inside
a wider piece of software like woofy is allowed. What it does **not**
permit is **standalone redistribution** — repackaging the asset on its
own, uploading it to another marketplace, or distributing it as a raw
asset pack.

If you fork this repository, build on it, or redistribute it as part of
your own project, that is permitted. If you strip out the model files
and republish them as a standalone asset, that is not.
